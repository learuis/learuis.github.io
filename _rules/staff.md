### 5.1 - BAND OF OUTCASTS STAFF CODE OF CONDUCT

Our Staff consists of the Admin team, Moderator Team, and Helper Team. This is our commitment to the players of this
server.

- The role of Staff is to help the community and create a positive environment.
- Staff will treat players and each other with respect.
- Staff will record the usage of admin powers in an admin-log.
- Staff will apply rules equally and fairly to themselves and all players on the server.
- Staff may perform admin duties on their own player characters, but will not use admin powers for their own benefit
  while doing so.
- Staff will not spawn items or NPCs for themselves or others to exploit the admin powers.
- Staff will not use creative mode / fly to build their own bases.
- Staff will not use admin powers / god mode to kill NPCs to clear dungeons or to be looted by others
- Staff will not process admin shop / community boon exchanges for themselves or their clan members.

### 5.2 - ADMIN DUTIES

Please be mindful that being an admin, moderator, or helper on this server is an unpaid, volunteer gig. We do not
enforce Staff "coverage", "shifts", or set any requirements for how much time they should spend doing admin duties vs
playing. Ideally, we want Staff to be able to spend most of their time playing the game. Admin duties are "as-needed,
as-willing". This is our leisure time as well, and we're doing our best. Please be patient and understanding.
