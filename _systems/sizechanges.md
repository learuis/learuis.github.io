On our server, we have the capability to change the size of some placeables, pets, and thralls, as well as players!

Structures can be shrunk down as small as 0.3x. Some examples:
- Map Room
- Small Animal Pen
- Stables
- T1-T3 Large Animal Pens 
- T1-T3 Wheels of Pain
- T3 Shrines

You must place the structure in the world first at full size. After shrinking, you can trim down support for the size (accurate to visible model)
Map Rooms at 0.5x take up a 4x4 if at the intersection of foundations. , 0.3x take up a 3x3 if at the center of a foundation.

The following entities can be shrunk or grown to either 0.75 or 1.25: 
- Thralls
- Pets
- Mounts
  - Mounts shrunk to 0.75 generally cannot be mounted and may sink in the floor if you somehow manage to do it :frowning: Sorry!
Horses and pets that are placed while you are shrunk will be placed as the same scale as you, but thralls will still be 1.0
- Player Character
  - Changing your character's size may have unintended consequences. You may find that you cannot enter some doors or access some parts of the map. If you encounter any problems, you can return to normal size by using an Orb of Nergal.
  - 
- Shrinking must be requested in advance in #❔support-and-suggestions❔ using the below template
- Shrinking will only occur during scheduled server restarts and is dependent on Verama's availability, with no guarantee of timing.
- Changing size of a placeable, pet, or thrall costs 1 Bronze Coin per request. This will be automatically deducted when the size change is complete.
- Player character size changes are free, as they can be done at any time.
- If you move a shrunken placeable after shrinking, it will return to normal size and you must request it again to re-shrink.
- Use **ALT+SHIFT+L** to copy and paste the approximate coordinates of the thing you want to shrink.
