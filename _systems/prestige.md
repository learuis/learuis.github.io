"Prestige" is a system for earning additional attribute points above and beyond the standard 60 available in vanilla. These are extra attribute points that you can spend as normal, starting at level 2. 
- Players earn Prestige points in two ways:
  - Registering for two consecutive seasons (beginning with 9). Each season boundary you cross where you registered in both will grant you 5 Prestige Points
  - Turning in Keystones to moderators, **1 Keystone = 1 Prestige Point** (DO NOT EAT THE KEYSTONE, THIS DELETES YOUR CHARACTER!)
- To be granted these additional points in-game, use the bot command `v/prestige`. 
- Because of the way attributes work, there will be situations where you end up with negative attribute points if you're under level 60. To address this, you can claim free respec potions with `v/respec`, until level 60.
- Extra attribute points won't be able to raise an attribute over 20, but it allows you to branch into additional trees.
