"Community Boons" is a server-wide system to improve certain server settings for 3 hours at a time.

There are 6 Boons currently available, each requiring a different item to activate.

- #### Boon of Blood (Experience Multiplier 0.5 -> 1.0) Heart of a Hero
- #### Boon of Abundance (Harvest Amount 1.0 -> 1.5) Tablet of Derketo
- #### Boon of Proliferation (NPC Respawn 1.0 -> 0.7) Skull of Yog 
- #### Boon of Regrowth (Resource Respawn 1.0 -> 1.5) Heart of Nordheimer
- #### Boon of Finesse (Stamina Cost 1.0 -> 0.8) Molten Heart 
- #### Boon of Returning (v/home Discount 100 -> 50) Eye of Set 

Activating these Boons requires turning in the requested item at the Profession Hub by standing on the designated target spot. Look for journals near each one to see which item to turn in there.

Once activated, each boon is broadcasted to the server and will last for 3 hours. Additional active boon time can be banked by turning in additional items.

Use the command `v/booninfo` to see the current status of boons!
